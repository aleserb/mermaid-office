Mermaid Office privacy policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Mermaid Office is a Microsoft Word add-in published by Aleksandr Serbin (GitHub: aleserb). It renders Mermaid source into editable diagram pictures. This policy covers the add-in and its public information pages, not only this website.

Document content stays in your editing environment

Rendering takes place locally in the add-in's browser or Office webview. Mermaid Office does not operate an application server, require a separate account, or send your document or Mermaid source to a rendering service. The publisher does not automatically receive your document content.

The add-in requests Word's ReadWriteDocument permission to inspect the selection and diagram metadata, insert and replace pictures and their content controls, and save editable source and settings in the document. Source, a generated diagram identifier, and rendering settings are stored in Word document settings and embedded in generated PNG pictures. Legacy diagrams can also contain source in SVG metadata.

Sharing a diagram can share its source. People who receive a document or an original diagram image may recover the embedded source, even without this add-in. Do not put secrets in diagram source on the assumption that only the visible picture will be shared.

Word, OneDrive, SharePoint, or another storage provider may save, sync, share, or back up your document according to your settings and their policies. Those services are separate from Mermaid Office.

Local preferences and temporary data

The add-in stores your preferred diagram theme and rendering settings in browser/webview local storage, using the keys mermaid-office:preferred-theme and mermaid-office:preferred-settings. These preferences are used for new diagrams; diagram source is not stored in these preference keys. Unsaved edits and working render data are held in memory.

Mermaid Office does not add advertising, analytics, tracking cookies, or automatic crash-report uploads. Diagnostic messages may appear locally in the browser or webview console. Review and redact logs before sharing them.

Network requests and third-party services

An internet connection is normally needed to load the add-in and its assets from GitHub Pages and Office.js from Microsoft's CDN. These providers may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser information, requested URL, and access time under their own policies. The publisher does not use these requests to build advertising profiles.

Links to Mermaid documentation and GitHub open external services. If your Mermaid source references external resources, such as remotely hosted images, the renderer or browser may request those URLs. The resource host can receive technical request information and information included in the URL. Avoid confidential data in external resource URLs.

For those services, see the GitHub privacy statement and the Microsoft privacy statement. Microsoft may also process Office usage and diagnostic data independently of the add-in.

Information you provide for support

If you open a GitHub issue, the publisher can see your GitHub identity and whatever information you submit. This information is used to respond, diagnose problems, and maintain the add-in. Issues and attachments in the public repository are public; a GitHub account is needed to submit an issue. Do not post private documents, credentials, confidential diagram source, or personal information. Use a minimal, non-sensitive example instead.

Support discussions may remain in the public issue history. The publisher does not sell support information or use it for advertising. GitHub controls its own retention, account processing, and infrastructure, which may involve processing in countries other than yours.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local preferences remain until you clear site data in the browser or Office webview. Unsaved in-memory edits are not persisted by the add-in and may be lost when the pane is closed or reloaded. Removing the add-in does not remove diagram source already stored in documents or images.

Saved metadata follows the lifetime of your documents, images, versions, backups, and shared copies. Some document metadata is retained for Undo and source recovery even after a picture is deleted. Removing a visible picture alone is not a guarantee that all source metadata is gone. Control or delete the relevant files and copies through your document storage provider when complete removal is needed. The publisher cannot access or delete those copies for you.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you supplied for support, or to object to its use. Contact the publisher through the support issue tracker with a non-sensitive request or a link to the relevant issue. Do not post identity documents or the information you want removed. GitHub may need to handle requests concerning its own systems. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Policy changes and contact

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Previous versions are available in the repository history. For questions, visit the Mermaid Office support page.
